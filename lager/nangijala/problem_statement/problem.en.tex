\problemname{Nangijala}
In Astrid Lindgren's novel \emph{The Brothers Lionheart}, one arrives in Nangijala after death. If one dies in Nangijala, one arrives in Nangilima. In Nangilima one cannot die and everyone lives in harmony, but one could imagine that there are more worlds beyond Nangilima.

In this problem, there are infinitely many worlds numbered 1, 2, 3, \dots. All people are originally in world 1 and when someone dies in world $i$ they arrive in world $i+1$.

Right now there are $N$ people in world 1. Among these people, there are $M$ pairs of enemies. Enemies dislike each other so much that they would prefer to be in different worlds. Enmity is a symmetric relation, which means that if person $a$ is an enemy of person $b$, then $b$ is also an enemy of $a$.

Determine the minimum number of deaths required so that no person is in the same world as any of their enemies.

\section*{Input}
The first line contains the positive integers $N$ and $M$.
Then follow $M$ lines with integers $a_i$, $b_i$ $(0 \le a_i, b_i < N, a_i \neq b_i)$ meaning that $a_i$ and $b_i$ are enemies.

\section*{Output}
Output a single number --- the minimum number of deaths needed so that no enemies are in the same world.
