\problemname{Form Words}

Fatimeh is studying her native language which uses the Arabic alphabet. Right now
she is working on an exercise where she must answer
how many ways she can form a word using the given letters in the task.

If it were in Swedish, the exercise could look like this:

\vspace{0.2in}
\centerline{r M e a}
\vspace{0.2in}

Since four letters are given, Fatimeh knows she must try $4*3*2*1 =
24$ permutations. But since the letter M is ``capital'', we know
that it must be placed at the beginning of the word. With that condition, only $6$
words can be formed, for example $Mera$, but
not $raMe$. In the Arabic alphabet, one does not have capital and lowercase letters
in the same way, but there are other rules about where
in the word a letter can appear, also in relation to other
letters.

In this problem, we assume there are two types of restrictions: Either a letter must come right before
some other letter, or a letter can only be at certain
positions. Examples of these rules and the notation we use are in
the following table:
\\
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|} \hline
\textbf{Rule} & \textbf{Notation} \\ \hline
Letter $B$ must be at position $1$ or position $4$ & \texttt{B@01,04}
\\ \hline
Letter $D$ must directly precede $C$ or $B$  & \texttt{D:CB} \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\\
Write a program that calculates
how many ways $N$ different letters (for simplicity called A,
B, C,... etc.) can be arranged, given a number of rules of these two
types.

\section*{Input}

On the first line are two integers, the number of letters $N$
and the number of rules $K$. Then follow $K$ lines each describing
a rule according to the notation above. A certain letter cannot appear first in more than one rule of each type. Note that all position numbers are written with two digits.

\section*{Output}

The program shall output one integer: the number of ways the letters can
be arranged. The answer will always be less than 10 million.

\section*{Scoring}

For test cases worth $60$ points, $2\le N\le 9$. For full
points ($100$ points), your program must handle $2\le N\le 15$. In all cases, $1\le K\le N$.
For large $N$, the restrictions will be roughly evenly distributed among
the letters.

\section*{Explanation of Sample 1}

The words that can be formed are ACDB, ADCB, BADC, CADB,
DCAB, and BDCA.
